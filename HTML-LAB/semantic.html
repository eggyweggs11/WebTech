Introduction to Web Development

Web development is the process of creating, building, and maintaining websites and web applications that run over the internet. It ranges from creating a simple text-only webpage to developing complex social network applications, e-commerce stores, and online tools.

The Importance of Web Development Skills

In today’s hyper-connected world, web development has transformed from a specialized technical craft into the absolute backbone of the global digital economy. Whether you are an entrepreneur launching a startup, a creative showcasing a portfolio, or a professional aiming to stay competitive, understanding how the web is built offers an unmatched strategic advantage.

World's First Website

The world's first website, info.cern.ch, went live on August 6, 1991. It was created by British scientist Sir Tim Berners-Lee at the CERN research institute in Switzerland using a NeXT computer.
